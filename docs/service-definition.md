# Service Definition

## One-Line Definition
ForFortune helps Korean users get approachable AI fortune readings, dream interpretations, and paid report/chat experiences through a web service connected to PortOne payments.

## Persona
Primary users are mobile-first Korean visitors who arrive from search, social, or shared links because they want quick but emotionally satisfying guidance about yearly fortune, dream interpretation, love, money, work, naming, travel, or similar everyday decisions.

## Core Pain
Users are curious enough to click, but they leave when the entry path is unclear, the result feels generic, the payment step feels risky, or the service does not quickly prove why a paid report or chat is worth continuing.

## Desired Transformation
Before:
- A visitor lands on a fortune or dream page, cannot tell what to do next, and drops before signup or payment.

After:
- A visitor understands the offer, tries the right fortune flow, signs in when needed, and can safely complete payment and receive a trustworthy result.

## Core Mechanism
SEO/GEO content attracts intent-driven visitors, clear fortune entry flows turn them into users, backend payment/session/document contracts keep the paid experience reliable, and admin analytics expose where the funnel breaks.
